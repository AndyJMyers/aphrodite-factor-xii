# Agent Pitch

## *Aphrodite Factor XII*
### *How Gods, Goddesses and Sorceresses Get Their Own Way — and How You Can Too*

**By Andy J Myers**  
**Commercial narrative non-fiction / humorous psychology**  
**Estimated length: 35,000–45,000 words, illustrated gift-book potential**

*Aphrodite Factor XII* is a mischievous, psychologically literate guide to status, seduction, social influence and the art of obtaining better invitations. It begins with an unfashionable proposition: human beings have not changed much since Ancient Greece. We still crave admiration, belonging, distinction, romance, favourable gossip, a subsidised drink and reliable help when something heavy needs moving.

The difference is that the Greeks understood the game well enough to turn it into mythology.

Across twelve chapters, the book uses gods, goddesses, heroes and sorceresses as case studies in influence. Eris throws the golden apple into a wedding and creates desire through comparison. Circe alters the terms of an encounter. The Sirens flatter intellectual vanity. Penelope wins through patience, ambiguity and superb reputation management. Calypso offers Odysseus the most seductive thing imaginable: an excuse not to make the difficult journey home.

The narrative is conducted by **Dionysus**—patron of pleasure, appetite and social electricity—and **Penelope**—mistress of the long game, standards and controlled disclosure. Their ongoing disagreement gives the book its comic engine. Dionysus sees every social problem as potentially soluble with atmosphere, wine and nerve; Penelope points out the costs, the collateral damage and the very real danger of waking up with one’s reputation on fire.

This is neither a dry retelling of the myths nor earnest pop psychology in a toga. The real insights—reciprocity, scarcity, social proof, emotional contagion, curiosity, self-presentation and strategic restraint—are delivered through a playful ancient world of symposiums, sea voyages, gods behaving badly and catastrophic romantic choices. Each chapter ends with a comic oracle: the reader’s **Aphrodite Factor**, **Wine Subsidy Index**, **Amphora-Carrying Assistance Rating**, **Madness Residue**, and a divine warning.

The book will appeal to readers who enjoy the accessible mythic intelligence of Stephen Fry’s *Mythos*, the irreverent social candour of Mark Manson, and the gift-book pleasure of a beautifully designed volume that can be read straight through or sampled at a dangerous moment before dinner.

It is designed to be visually rich: chapter-opening Greek tableaux, classical marginal motifs, oracle-style scorecards and a premium, giftable cover. It has clear potential for excerpting and social sharing: “The Sirens’ Song: Flatter Their Cleverness,” “Penelope’s Loom: Delay Is a Weapon,” and “Competence Is Foreplay” are all self-contained hooks.

Andy J Myers is a writer, artist, musician and retired software engineer based in Surrey. He publishes frequently on Substack and has built an extensive body of essays, fiction, poetry, music and visual work. *Aphrodite Factor XII* brings together his interest in mythology, social observation, comedy and the abiding absurdity of human behaviour.
